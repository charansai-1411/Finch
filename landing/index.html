Questions

Everything you'd ask before pasting.

Do I need a developer?
No. If you can paste a line of text into your site's header or an embed box, you can install Finch. That's the whole job — no coding, no rebuild.
Which platforms does it work on?
Any site that lets you add HTML — Shopify, WooCommerce/WordPress, Wix, Squarespace, custom builds, or via Google Tag Manager. The one-line snippet is platform-agnostic by design.
How does it learn my catalog?
Upload a CSV or paste your store URL. Finch indexes every product automatically — no schema, no tagging, no configuration.
Will it make up products or prices?
No. Finch is grounded strictly in your catalog and confirms price and stock from your source of truth before quoting them. If nothing fits, it says so honestly.
Is the embed key safe on a public page?
The key is publishable, like Stripe's or Intercom's. It's scoped to your catalog and protected by a domain allowlist and rate limiting, so it only works on your site.
Does it handle checkout?
Finch takes shoppers all the way to add-to-cart. Checkout stays in your existing store flow — Finch guides the sale, your store closes it.
